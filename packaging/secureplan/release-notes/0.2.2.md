SecurePlan CAD 0.2.2 shows the SecurePlan design as it looks in SecurePlan, lets you choose the device symbols for a CAD export and shows the progress of Apply. SecurePlan needs this version.

- The SecurePlan design overlay now draws devices with their SecurePlan icons, colours, labels and sizes, and camera coverage filled in the camera's colour, as SecurePlan's canvas does. Custom uploaded icons are shown too, up to 4096 pixels a side. An icon that cannot be read, or an SVG icon that uses effects such as filters, masks, clipping or text, shows the standard symbol instead. When you zoom far out, devices become small dots and labels too small to read are left out, so large plans stay fast.
- The CAD export dialog has a new choice, Device symbols: SecurePlan icons (the default) or Standard symbols. SecurePlan icons become blocks with the device colour, icon and badge, with the label below the symbol as in SecurePlan. Either choice draws each device and its label at the device's symbol scale. Devices with an uploaded picture as their icon keep the standard symbol, and the export summary says so.
- Apply now shows a progress window from start to finish: preparing the drawing, creating the PDF and the snap file, sending to SecurePlan with the amount sent, waiting while you confirm in SecurePlan, and SecurePlan's saving steps. It ends with the version applied, or with the reason Apply stopped, and Close. You can cancel while the drawing, PDF and snap file are being prepared; nothing is sent then. If SecurePlan asks to reconnect the drawing during Apply, the progress window returns once you answer.

Install: on macOS, open the disk image, move SecurePlan CAD to Applications, approve it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and open it once. On Windows, run the installer and accept the SmartScreen warning. Installed copies offer this update themselves.
