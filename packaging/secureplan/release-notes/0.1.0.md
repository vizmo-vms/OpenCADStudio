First release of SecurePlan CAD, the desktop CAD companion of SecurePlan.

- Pairs with the SecurePlan web page (Chrome or Edge) over an authenticated, encrypted loopback connection after you trust the website.
- Imports, aligns and edits a survey's DWG or DXF drawing, and applies it to SecurePlan as a vector PDF, a snap file and the drawing.
- Publishes a paper layout, converts selected CAD lines into SecurePlan walls or routes, shows the SecurePlan design read-only, and exports CAD with the design.
- Checks this page for updates at startup and daily (you can turn this off), and installs an update after you choose Update.

Install: on macOS, open the disk image, move SecurePlan CAD to Applications, approve it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and open it once. On Windows, run the installer and accept the SmartScreen warning.
