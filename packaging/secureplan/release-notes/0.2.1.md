SecurePlan CAD 0.2.1 fixes Apply for drawings with rounded hatches, makes aligning a drawing easier and keeps to one window.

- Align has a new Measure in drawing button. Pick the two ends of a known length, such as a dimension or a wall, and SecurePlan CAD fills in "Known length in the drawing" and switches to calibrating. Then type its real length. You can also type the points at the command line, and Escape brings the dialog back unchanged. It measures model space, so use the Model tab or a layout viewport.
- Choices in the SecurePlan dialogs are now dropdowns. With the keyboard, Left and Right still change the focused choice.
- Apply no longer refuses drawings whose hatches follow clockwise arcs, such as rounded corners and door swings, with "A curve in the published view is too large". If a curve really is too large to publish, the message now says where it is in the drawing.
- Only one SecurePlan CAD window opens. Starting SecurePlan CAD again brings the open window to the front.

Install: on macOS, open the disk image, move SecurePlan CAD to Applications, approve it once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and open it once. On Windows, run the installer and accept the SmartScreen warning. Installed copies offer this update themselves.
